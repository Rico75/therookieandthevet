Main Hosts

Rico

Rico

Rico brings a gambler’s instinct, a strategist’s brain, and a fan’s heart to The Rookie And The Vet Podcast. Known for spotting momentum shifts before they happen, he digs into the stories behind the stats — the grit, the psychology, the chaos — to reveal why the “long shots” are often the smartest plays. If you love bold takes, sharp breakdowns, and rooting for the underdog, you’re in the right place.

Ivan

Ivan

My name is Ivan and I’m younger than my two co hosts by at least 15 years. I like traveling to away games for my beloved Steelers at least two or three times a year. I enjoy keeping an eye on the younger players and players trying to make the roster because I feel like they’re hungrier. Lifelong wrestling fan and I can bring the flash and WOOOO!!! Like Ric Flair. I paint my face, wear wrestling masks, and lose my voice constantly while watching sports.

Ben

Ben

Ben (aka MrBen) is a lifelong sports fan and co-host of The Rookie And The Vet Podcast. A former Marine with a soft spot for underdogs, he brings heat and data to every debate—covering major pro sports, fantasy football, and combat sports with equal parts passion and zero filter. Open to rooting for (and roasting) any team, MrBen is always ready to ride with the Dark Horse.
